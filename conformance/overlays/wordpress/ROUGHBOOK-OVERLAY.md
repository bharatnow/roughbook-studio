# wordpress

Existing pages for the `wordpress` fixture (ROUGHBOOK-BROWNFIELD-PLAN.md, B1), laid over it by
`conformance/run.mjs`: a front page made of two patterns and a newsletter part between the theme's
header and footer, and page and single-post templates, which a fresh WordPress answers at
`/?page_id=2` (its sample page) and `/?p=1` (its first post). Edit it by hand: no scaffold writes
it.
