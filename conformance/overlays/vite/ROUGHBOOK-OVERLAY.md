# vite

An existing page for the `vite` fixture (ROUGHBOOK-BROWNFIELD-PLAN.md, B5b), laid over it by
`conformance/run.mjs`: an app with no router, so one page at `/`, whose sections are a hero, a
pricing section that gets the app's plans as props, and an FAQ, each a component of its own under
`src/components/`. The pricing section is adopted and shown in its page with an iteration the
studio copies from the app. Edit it by hand: no scaffold writes it.
